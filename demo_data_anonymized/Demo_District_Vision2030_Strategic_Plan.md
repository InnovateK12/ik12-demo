# VISION 2030 Strategic Plan
## Excellence for All — A Future-Ready Education Community

**Prepared For:** Greenfield Unified School District  
**Planning Horizon:** 2026–2030  
**Framework:** Strategic Planning & Implementation Roadmap  

---

## Executive Summary

Greenfield Unified School District (GUSD) is committed to creating a future-ready educational community where every student is prepared for success in enrollment, employment, enlistment, and lifelong learning.

The VISION 2030 Strategic Plan establishes a coherent system of teaching, learning, leadership, innovation, and support that ensures every student has access to rigorous instruction, meaningful relationships, and future-focused opportunities.

The plan is organized around four strategic priority areas:

1. Academic Achievement & Postsecondary Readiness
2. Safe, Supportive & Inclusive Learning Environments
3. Supported Workforce
4. Future-Ready Learning & Innovation

Implementation is guided through phased development, continuous improvement cycles, stakeholder collaboration, and measurable outcomes.

---

## Vision

GUSD will cultivate a future-ready educational community where every learner is empowered to think critically, communicate effectively, solve complex problems, and contribute meaningfully to an ever-changing world.

---

## Mission

To provide equitable, engaging, and high-quality learning experiences that prepare all students for success in college, career, military service, and civic life.

---

## Core Beliefs

- Every student can learn at high levels.
- Literacy and mathematics are foundational gateways to future success.
- Innovation must enhance—not replace—high-quality instruction.
- Diversity strengthens learning communities.
- Data should inform decisions and accelerate student growth.
- Strong relationships are essential to student achievement.
- Professional learning drives organizational excellence.
- Continuous improvement is a shared responsibility.

# Strategic Priority Area 1: Academic Achievement & Postsecondary Readiness

## Purpose

Ensure every student engages in rigorous, real-world learning experiences that foster intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, and student ownership while preparing them for enrollment, employment, or enlistment.

## Strategic Objectives

### Objective 1.1: Establish Instructional Coherence
Establish a coherent K–12 instructional system aligned to high-quality instructional materials, clear instructional expectations, and consistent professional learning structures.

### Objective 1.2: Strengthen Literacy Achievement
Increase literacy proficiency through evidence-based instruction, aligned intervention systems, and clear K–12 readiness progressions.

### Objective 1.3: Strengthen Mathematics Achievement
Improve mathematics achievement through vertically aligned learning progressions, protected instructional time, and thinking-centered instruction.

### Objective 1.4: Expand Postsecondary Readiness
Expand workforce-aligned pathways, Career and Technical Education opportunities, dual enrollment, and credentialing opportunities connected to enrollment, employment, or enlistment.

# Strategic Priority Area 2: Safe, Supportive & Inclusive Learning Environments

## Purpose

Create physically and emotionally safe environments that foster strong relationships, belonging, consistent behavior expectations, and equitable access to support and opportunity.

## Strategic Objectives

### Objective 2.1: Strengthen Safety Systems
Create a proactive school safety model through trained School Safety Ambassadors and consistent preparedness systems.

### Objective 2.2: Expand Student Support Systems
Increase access to timely, targeted academic, behavioral, language, and social-emotional supports.

### Objective 2.3: Increase Family Engagement
Build trust and connection through accessible family engagement opportunities and community programming.

### Objective 2.4: Reduce Barriers to Participation
Expand afterschool transportation and access to enrichment, academic support, clubs, athletics, and extracurricular opportunities.

# Strategic Planning Dashboard Requirements

The executive dashboard should include:

- Overall plan progress
- Priority area progress
- Initiative health status
- Budget status
- Major milestones
- Student outcome trends
- Workforce indicators
- Community engagement indicators

## Initiative Dashboard

Each initiative dashboard should include:

- Strategic lead
- Current phase
- Completion percentage
- Key deliverables
- KPI status
- Timeline status
- Budget status
- Risk level
- Next major milestone

## Community Dashboard

The community dashboard should include:

- Priority area summaries
- Public progress updates
- Major accomplishments
- Student outcome trends
- Family engagement opportunities
- Annual reports

Do not include:

- Student-level data
- Personnel records
- Confidential budget records
- Internal risk registers
