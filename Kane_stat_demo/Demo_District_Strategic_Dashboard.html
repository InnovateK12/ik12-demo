About this dashboard. The Lakewood REA 2026–2029 Strategic Dashboard monitors progress on five core organizational priorities through 12 enterprise KPIs. It tracks both internal workforce health and external partner experience. The first year (2026) establishes baseline data and measurement systems. Starting in 2027, results are tracked against interim benchmarks, with final targets set for 2029. Once data is collected, each indicator will show green, yellow, or red — giving leadership, staff, and partners a clear, honest picture of progress.

Dashboard Design Principles

7 guiding principles from the strategic plan
1
Use a small number of high-value indicators — focus on what matters most.
2
Track both internal workforce health and external partner experience.
3
Establish 2026 as the baseline year — the starting point for all measurements.
4
Use 2027 as the early implementation benchmark — first checkpoint for progress.
5
Use 2029 as the strategic target year — the end-of-plan goal for all KPIs.
6
Review quarterly where possible; annually for all survey-based measures.
7
Report results using simple color status indicators: green, yellow, red, gray.

Recommended Board Reporting Format

Structure for each quarterly or annual report

One-page executive dashboard
High-level snapshot of all 12 KPIs with current status indicators (green / yellow / red / gray), current results vs. targets, and trend direction.
2
Strategic priority summary

KPI trend table

6

Specific, time-bound commitments for what leadership will do to address lagging indicators before the next review.

Draft Board Dashboard Narrative

The Lakewood REA 2026–2029 Strategic Dashboard is designed to monitor progress on the organization's core strategic priorities. The dashboard includes a focused set of enterprise indicators that measure workforce sustainability, professional learning, leadership trust, organizational communication, cross-department collaboration, and external partner satisfaction.

The first year of implementation will establish baseline data and clarify measurement systems. Beginning in 2027, the dashboard will be used to monitor progress toward interim benchmarks and long-term 2029 targets. This approach ensures the strategic plan remains active, measurable, and responsive to staff and partner feedback.

5 phases · Summer 2026 → 2029

  • Assign KPI owners across departments

  • Establish baseline calculation methods and collection procedures

  • Administer partner survey and collect responses

  • Finalize 2026 baseline values for all 12 KPIs
  • Set final numerical targets for 2027 and 2029
'27

  • Review first progress update with Cabinet leadership
  • Identify areas requiring action planning and additional resources
  • Publish simplified Board dashboard with initial results
  • Adjust initiatives and strategies based on early data
'28

Midpoint Review
  • Compare current results to 2027 benchmarks and 2029 targets
  • Identify priorities on track, lagging, or exceeding expectations
  • Refine initiatives and reallocate resources where needed
  • Share midpoint report with board and stakeholders
'29
2029 · Strategic Target Year
Final Evaluation
  • Evaluate final progress against all 12 KPI targets
  • Identify achieved goals and remaining gaps
  • Celebrate wins and recognize staff contributions
