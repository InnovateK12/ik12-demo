# Lakewood REA 2026-2029 Strategic Dashboard Build

## Purpose

This markdown file provides a practical structure for building a strategic dashboard aligned to the Lakewood REA 2026-2029 Strategic Framework. It is designed for use in a spreadsheet, BI dashboard, board reporting template, or internal progress monitoring system.

The recommended dashboard uses 12 enterprise KPIs organized across five strategic priorities:

1. Financial Sustainability & Workforce Investment
2. Professional Learning & Organizational Growth
3. Leadership, Trust & Organizational Alignment
4. Communication & Stakeholder Engagement
5. Thriving Workforce, Culture & Collaboration

---

## Dashboard Design Principles

- Use a small number of high-value indicators.
- Track both internal workforce health and external partner experience.
- Establish 2026 as the baseline year.
- Use 2027 as an early implementation benchmark.
- Use 2029 as the strategic target year.
- Review dashboard data quarterly where possible and annually for survey-based measures.
- Report results using simple color status indicators: green, yellow, red.

---

## Status Definitions

| Status | Definition |
|---|---|
| Green | On track or meeting/exceeding target |
| Yellow | Progressing but below target or needs attention |
| Red | Not progressing, declining, or requires action |
| Gray | Baseline not yet established or data unavailable |

## Recommended KPI Definitions

### 1. Employee Retention Rate

**Strategic Priority:** Financial Sustainability & Workforce Investment  
**Definition:** Percentage of employees who remain employed from the beginning to the end of the reporting year.  
**Calculation:** `(Number of employees retained / Number of employees at start of year) x 100`  
**Data Source:** HR records  
**Review Cycle:** Quarterly and annual  
**2026 Baseline:** Establish baseline  
**2027 Benchmark:** Improve by 3%  
**2029 Target:** Improve by 8%

---

### 2. Employee Engagement Index

**Strategic Priority:** Thriving Workforce, Culture & Collaboration  
**Definition:** Composite score from staff survey items related to engagement, belonging, morale, and commitment.  
**Calculation:** Average favorable response rate across selected engagement items.  
**Data Source:** Annual staff survey  
**Review Cycle:** Annual  
**2026 Baseline:** Establish baseline  
**2027 Benchmark:** Improve by 10%  
**2029 Target:** Improve by 20%

Recommended survey items:

- I enjoy working at Lakewood REA.
- I would recommend Lakewood REA as a place to work.
- I feel connected to the mission of the organization.
- I feel supported in my role.
- I feel a sense of belonging at Lakewood REA.

## Implementation Timeline

### Summer 2026: Dashboard Setup

- Confirm final list of enterprise KPIs.
- Assign KPI owners.
- Build staff and partner survey items aligned to dashboard indicators.
- Identify available HR, professional learning, and operational data sources.
- Establish baseline calculation methods.

### Fall 2026: Baseline Collection

- Collect staff survey data.
- Collect partner survey data.
- Compile HR and professional learning records.
- Finalize 2026 baseline values.
- Set final 2027 and 2029 numerical targets.

### Winter / Spring 2027: First Progress Review

- Review first progress update with Cabinet.
- Identify areas requiring action planning.
- Publish simplified Board dashboard.
- Adjust initiatives as needed.

### 2028: Midpoint Review

- Compare current results to 2027 benchmarks and 2029 targets.
- Identify strategic priorities that are on track, lagging, or exceeding expectations.
- Refine initiatives and resource allocation.

### 2029: Final Evaluation

- Evaluate final progress against 2029 targets.
- Identify achieved goals and remaining gaps.
- Use results to inform the next strategic planning cycle.

## Draft Board Dashboard Narrative

The Lakewood REA 2026-2029 Strategic Dashboard is designed to monitor progress on the organization's core strategic priorities. The dashboard includes a focused set of enterprise indicators that measure workforce sustainability, professional learning, leadership trust, organizational communication, cross-department collaboration, and external partner satisfaction.

The first year of implementation will establish baseline data and clarify measurement systems. Beginning in 2027, the dashboard will be used to monitor progress toward interim benchmarks and long-term 2029 targets. This approach ensures the strategic plan remains active, measurable, and responsive to staff and partner feedback.
