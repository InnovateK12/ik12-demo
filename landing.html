What We Deliver

InnovateK12 leverages and optimizes the science of human interaction and innovation to deliver more dynamic, effective solutions for school districts — customized, short-cycle, synthesized analytics at scale, organized under five core services.

◈
Data Dashboards & Analytics
Predictive and forecasting analytics, data integrity improvements, elimination of data silos, and shorter-cycle reporting than traditional lagging indicators.
◷
Strategic Planning
Board orientation, survey analytics, and efficient processes built around continuous improvement — typically a 4–6 month engagement from pre-planning through execution.
◫
Progress Monitoring
KPI and metrics development and measurement, with dual internal and external dashboards so every stakeholder sees the right view of progress.
✦
Stakeholder Voice
Large-scale strategic engagement and qualitative data collection from internal and external stakeholders, synthesized into cogent, actionable reporting.
⌬
AI Insights
Ask natural-language questions about your district's data — enrollment, proficiency, attendance, or spend — and get instant, accurate answers sourced directly from your District Hub.
