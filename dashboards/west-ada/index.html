Safety & Behavior

Report p.10

West Ada strengthened proactive student support in 2024–25 through clearer discipline tracking, expanded staffing, and a new district-wide behavior framework.

652 Support Log Entries, by Level

🛡️ New this year

  • New SRO for northern elementary schools, funded through a state grant
  • New Coordinator of Safety and Behavior and Behavior Consultant, addressing 650+ support needs
  • New Student Behavior Support Framework and PBS training rolled out district-wide
  • Improved discipline tracking through PowerSchool; monthly scenario-based PD for assistant principals

District-wide safety audits and lockdown drills were conducted to keep sites aligned on protocol.
