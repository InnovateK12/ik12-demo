Accountability

How Progress Will Be Monitored

SCCPSS uses a three-tier measurement system to connect public outcomes, ongoing progress, and internal execution.

Tier 1

Goals

Public-facing student outcome goals that define success for the district.

Tier 2

Progress Indicators

Ongoing measures that show whether the district is on track.

Tier 3

Internal Performance Measures

Operational and implementation measures used to monitor execution and improve performance.

The structure is designed to measure impact, not just activity.

The Impact

What Will Be Different

For Students and Families

  • More consistent, high-quality learning across schools
  • Stronger literacy and numeracy
  • Greater access to pathways and real-world experiences
  • Safer and more supportive environments

For Staff

  • Clearer expectations
  • Stronger coaching and professional learning
  • Better aligned support systems
  • A more stable and valued workforce

For the Community

  • Stronger academic outcomes
  • Greater transparency and trust
  • More confidence that every school delivers a high-quality experience
  • Graduates better prepared for college, careers, and choice-filled lives
