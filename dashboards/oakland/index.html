LCFF Budget Overview for Parents

Where the Money Comes From — and Goes

OUSD projects $806.5M in total revenue for 2025-26 and plans to spend $916.4M from its general fund. Of that spending, $356.9M is tied directly to LCAP actions and services; the remainder funds base operations — teachers, facilities, utilities, and other core functions.

    Equity Multiplier Schools

    15 sites receive targeted state Equity Multiplier funding ($4.5M across Goals 5–8) for schools with historically low academic performance. Each site's plan is individually scoped to its own community's needs.
