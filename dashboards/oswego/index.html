    What is driving facility need

    The same building systems recur across the district

    Every facility assessment names its five highest-cost projected needs. Counting how often each building system appears across all 22 facilities shows where need is systemic rather than site-specific.

    “Over 77% of anticipated budget is associated with HVAC, roofing, and flooring.”

    That statement appears in the Long-Range Facility Plan’s Capital Repairs and Infrastructure component. It is corroborated independently by the facility-level assessments shown here, which were compiled separately.

    Source: 2025 Long-Range Facility Plan

    Frequency reflects how often a system appears among a facility’s five highest projected capital needs. Counts are calculated at runtime from the 22 facility records — no percentages are entered by hand.

    Health & Life Safety

    Near-term safety obligations

    Illinois districts are required to survey every school building every ten years. SD 308’s decennial survey was completed in three phases — the two high schools in 2023, the five junior highs in 2024, and the fifteen elementary and specialty schools in 2025. The survey concluded that district facilities are in good condition overall.

    Source: 2025 10-Year Health & Life Safety Survey

    2025 Long-Range Facility Plan

    Updated facility assessments and the district priority matrix

    In December 2025 the district published building-by-building facility assessments for all 22 schools, followed by a proposed budget options matrix of named cost items across those buildings. This section presents that newer material in full, with the cost items grouped into three categories — Safety/Security, Critical Infrastructure and Other Improvements — per the district’s classification. It sits alongside the sections above rather than replacing them — the two sets of documents were produced separately and their figures are not interchangeable. Referendum project phasing has not been determined, so no sequence or timeline is implied by the ordering or grouping below.

    Sources: LRFP Volumes 2–4 (12/15/2025) · Proposed Budget Options (corrected 09/11/2026)
    i Two published cost bases. Each building’s own assessment page reports unescalated construction costs. Each volume’s summary table reports escalated construction costs rounded to the nearest $0.1M. Both are reproduced here and kept in separate fields; they are never added together or converted into one another. All figures are construction costs only and do not reflect total project costs.
