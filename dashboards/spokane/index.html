2026 Levy Transparency

Understanding the Replacement EP&O Levy

The Spokane School Board approved a resolution placing a replacement Educational Programs and Operations levy on the November 3, 2026 ballot. If approved, the levy would replace the current measure and provide collections for 2028, 2029, and 2030.

Proposed collection schedule — estimated rate and proposed amount by collection year

2028Estimated $2.50Proposed $107,000,000
2029Estimated $2.50Proposed $111,000,000

About these figures: All levy amounts shown are proposed, and the $2.50 rate is an estimate published by the district. This dashboard is informational and does not provide a voting recommendation.

For voter registration and Election Day details, contact Spokane County Elections directly.
