The Governance Wheel

The framework doesn't weight every behavior equally. Vision & Goals and Monitoring & Accountability carry two-thirds of the total score — because a board's goals and how it watches progress toward them matter more than how it talks about them. The wheel below is proportioned to those actual weights.

Six Competencies, One Standard

Each competency is scored on the same four-tier ladder — Not Focused, Approaching, Meeting, Mastering — but the point ceiling differs by how much that behavior tends to move student outcomes.

Where the Meeting Minutes Go
