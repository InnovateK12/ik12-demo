Goal 5 · Focus Goal

Equity Multiplier Schools

Four sites receive targeted state Equity Multiplier funding ($577,667 across Goal 5 actions) for schools with high student mobility (>25%) and socioeconomic disadvantage (>70%). By June 2027, each site targets a +3% attendance gain plus site-specific goals.

Crossroads High School

Identified 2024 · Continuation
  • Attendance improved 48.8% → 64.0%
  • On-pace graduation 52.2% → 50% (2024→26)
  • Target: +3% attendance & on-pace grad rate

Olympic High School

Identified 2024 · Continuation
  • Graduation rate 59% at qualification
  • Qualified for Comprehensive Support & Improvement
  • Target: +3% attendance & on-pace grad rate

Glenbrook Academy

Identified 2025
  • Small-population site — progress tracked with local data
  • Target: +3% attendance rate
  • Target: +3% on-pace graduation rate
